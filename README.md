# StudyFlow AI

An AI-powered productivity suite designed to help students study smarter, not harder. StudyFlow AI merges scattered notes into comprehensive summaries, manages focus with advanced timers, and generates personalized study schedules.

---

## Features

### NoteFusion AI
- **Multi-File Upload:** Upload PDFs, TXT, and PPTs simultaneously.
- **Smart Formatting:** Automatically extracts and formats Definitions, Formulas (with subscripts), and Theory.
- **Q&A Extraction:** Identifies questions in notes and formats them clearly.
- **PDF Generation:** Creates clean, downloadable study guides.

### Focus Zone
- **Dual Modes:** Choose between classic Pomodoro (25/5) or Custom Timers.
- **Audio Alarms:** Built-in Web Audio API alarms for study/break transitions.
- **Task Tracking:** Integrated To-Do list with motivational progress messages.

### Timetable Generator
- **Date Integration:** Automatically fetches today's date.
- **Exam Countdown:** Generates a day-by-day schedule from today until your exam date.
- **Priority Sorting:** Allocates more time to high-priority subjects.
